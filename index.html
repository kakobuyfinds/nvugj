Before you order through Sugargoo

Check the seller item

Open a CAFANS listing, choose Buy Link and select Sugargoo if available. If the link fails, try the original seller URL in the agent’s product search. Match the item and color before paying.

Confirm measurements

For shoes, check whether the size chart shows foot length or insole length. For clothing, compare garment measurements with something you already own. For bags, confirm the dimensions and strap length.

Review warehouse photos

If warehouse QC photos are available, check the size label, color, stitching and any marks on your selected variant. Check the exchange deadline before approving shipment.

Estimate your total order cost

Enter all amounts in USD. Use a shipping quote for your destination and packed parcel dimensions. Include each fee once if it is already part of a quote.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Sugargoo spreadsheet?

It is a product link directory that helps you compare listings before opening the item through a shopping agent. This page uses CAFANS listings with a dated price snapshot.

Where does Buy it now take me?

It opens the CAFANS homepage. From there, choose a product and check the shopping agent options on its product page.

Why might the checkout price differ?

The seller’s variant price, exchange rate, shipping quote and service fees can change. Use the amounts shown in your order and shipping quotes when planning your budget.
